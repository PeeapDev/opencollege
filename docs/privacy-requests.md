# Privacy-request procedure for OpenCollege operators

This is a process template for institutions using OpenCollege, not a statement
that all steps are automated by the software. Adapt it to applicable law and
publish the institution's own privacy contact and retention schedule.

1. **Receive and record.** Accept requests through the institution's published
   privacy contact. Record the date, request type, case owner, and deadline in
   a restricted register. For the reference deployment, requests may be sent
   to college.edu.sl@gmail.com for routing to the relevant institution.
2. **Verify safely.** Confirm the requester is the data subject or an
   authorised guardian or representative using an existing authenticated
   channel when possible. Ask only for the minimum extra information needed.
   Do not request passwords or full identity documents by ordinary email.
3. **Identify the records.** Check the live application and, where relevant,
   connected services, backups, and audit records. Limit access to staff who
   need to process the request. Do not run a whole-institution export as the
   default response to an individual request.
4. **Assess and act.** Correct inaccuracies; provide access or a copy through
   a secure channel; or delete/restrict information where permitted. Document
   any legal or academic-record retention reason for refusing or limiting a
   request. Do not promise that deleting an account erases every related record.
5. **Respond and close.** Explain the action taken, any limits, and how the
   requester can follow up or complain. Meet the deadline applicable to that
   institution and jurisdiction; record the response date and outcome. Retain
   the case record only as long as necessary under the operator's policy.

The maintainer should route requests about institution-held records to the
appropriate operator. The operator remains responsible for deciding the lawful
basis, verifying identity, and fulfilling the request. Before using any export
endpoint for personal data, verify its authorization and behaviour in the
deployed version. Public-repository code alone is not proof of deployment.
